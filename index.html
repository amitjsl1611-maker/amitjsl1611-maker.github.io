How I Work

Not a process.
A way of thinking.

Four principles that shape every engagement — from a first stakeholder conversation to a shipped product.

01

Earn the right to design

I spend more time understanding a problem than solving it. That means business constraints, market forces, domain knowledge, and the gap between what users say and what they actually do — before a single frame is made.

02

Fix the frame, not the form

Most product failures are framing failures. I work with stakeholders to distinguish root causes from symptoms, establish shared language, and redirect effort toward problems worth solving — even when that means questioning the brief.

03

Design for change

Products that scale are built on decisions, not deliverables. I think in systems — interaction models, information architecture, component logic — so that what gets built today doesn't become tomorrow's technical debt.

04

Close the loop

Shipping is not the end. I stay accountable to outcomes — testing assumptions, reading usage data, and refining until the product works in the real world, not just in a prototype.
